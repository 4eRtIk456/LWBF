## 🍄 About the Game

**Little Witch in Big Forest (LWBF)** is a story-driven, top-down pixel-art horror game about grief, memory, and learning to let go.

You play as **Phoebe**, a young witch who lives alone near an ancient forest and makes potions for a living. One day, a mysterious letter asks her to brew a potion that can bring back something lost. To gather the ingredients, she must return to a forest connected to the disappearance of her closest friend, **Ivy**.

The deeper Phoebe travels, the more the forest changes. Familiar places turn unsettling, memories become distorted, and a mysterious spirit begins to follow her. With only her magical lantern to protect her, Phoebe must face what she has tried to forget.

> **Can she find what was lost — or will the forest claim her too?**

## ✨ Planned Features

- **Explore an enchanted forest** — journey through areas that become darker and more mysterious as the story unfolds.
- **Collect potion ingredients** — investigate Phoebe's home and search the forest for unusual magical materials.
- **Use a sacred lantern** — manage light and defend yourself when hostile spirits approach.
- **Uncover lost memories** — find fragments of the past and learn more about Phoebe and Ivy.
- **Interact with the world** — examine objects, read Phoebe's diary, and discover details through exploration.
- **Experience different endings** — Phoebe's fate depends on how her journey unfolds.

*These are planned gameplay features; not all are implemented in the current prototype.*

## 🧙 Main Characters

**Phoebe** — A reserved young witch who brews potions and lives at the edge of the forest. She carries guilt over the disappearance of her friend and is reluctant to revisit the past.

**Ivy** — Phoebe's lively, mischievous friend, whose disappearance changed everything. Her story is closely connected to the secrets hidden in the forest.

## 🚀 Running the Project

1. Install **Unity Hub** and **Unity 6.3 LTS**.
2. Clone or download this repository.
3. In Unity Hub, choose **Add project from disk** and select the project folder.
4. Open the project and go to `Assets/Scenes/`.
5. Open the `Menu` scene (or a current prototype scene) and press **Play**.

> This is a work-in-progress Unity project, not yet a finished game release.
